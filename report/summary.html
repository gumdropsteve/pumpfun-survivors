Survivorship bias, on purpose

Everything about trader P&L here is measured in the tokens that survived. Even there, most positions are small and the big profits sit with a few wallets. Trades in the other 99.998% of tokens were not measured; most of those tokens are worth close to nothing.

What the X post got right

24.4M launches is correct. "312 above $100k" was low: we count 390 (and ~440 including early tokens without the "pump" suffix). PEPE and WIF never launched on pump.fun.
